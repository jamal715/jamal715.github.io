Energy markets / NCGCL / 2026

The price of a promise

What does it cost to fix electricity prices for a year—and who carries the risk when the reserve runs out?

I developed research and a simulation model using ISMO demand and marginal-cost profiles to examine a prefunded electricity-price guarantee for Pakistan. The model connects fixed-price choices, buyer contributions and reserve depletion to the guarantor’s residual exposure.

Read the research ↗
Price guarantees · Scenario simulation · Reserve sizing

What I built

  • Price and demand analysis to establish consumption-weighted benchmarks.
  • Historical replay and sensitivity analysis to compare fixed prices and reserve contributions.
  • Fund drawdown simulations to examine claims, reserve exhaustion and guarantor exposure.
  • A decision framework connecting buyer affordability with the capital needed to support the promise.

Research in progress. Historical scenarios are conditional on the data and model assumptions; they are not forecasts.
